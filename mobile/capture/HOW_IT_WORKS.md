# Rack Sweep Guide - How It Works

Open the link on your iPhone in Safari. It guides you through filming one
video of a server rack, top to bottom, so it can be stitched into one tall
photo. Every video you record is sent to the team automatically.

## How to use it

1. Open the link in Safari, not in Chrome and not from inside another app.
2. Type your name. The phone remembers it for next time.
3. Tap **Start camera**, then tap **Allow** for both **Camera** and
   **Motion & Orientation Access** when asked. If you deny motion access,
   the tilt and moving-up checks stay off for the whole sweep - the app
   will tell you, but it is better to allow it up front.
4. Hold your phone **sideways**, both hands.
5. Tap the **record button**, then **OK, start recording**.
6. Move slowly from the **top to the bottom** of the rack, keeping the
   same distance and staying centred.
7. Tap the record button again to **stop**.

While recording, the app shows a tip on screen and reads it aloud if you
are going too fast, too close, drifting sideways, tilted, or need more
light. Just follow the tip and keep going. (On Android phones it also
buzzes for serious problems. iPhones cannot buzz, so you will not see a
Buzz switch there.) It never stops the recording for you, unless you switch
away from the app mid-sweep - that ends the take early and flags it for a
reshoot.

## When you're done

- The video and its report are **sent to the team by themselves**. Watch
  the line under the result: it counts up and then says **Sent to team**.
  Wait for that before you leave the rack or close the page. If it says
  **Not sent**, tap that line to try again.
- **Passed the checks**: you are done. You do not need to save anything.
  To keep your own copy, tap **Save video**, then **Save Video** in the
  menu that opens. It goes to **Photos**, in the phone's own storage, so
  it works even when iCloud Drive is full.
- **Needs a reshoot**: the screen says why (too short, too many
  corrections, or interrupted). It is still sent to the team. Tap **Do
  another one** and retry.
