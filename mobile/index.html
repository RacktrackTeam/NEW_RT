    RackTrack reads a rack from one video: its units, devices, makes, models and ports.

    Connections

    The logins RackTrack uses to copy your NetBox and ServiceNow records in. Passwords and tokens are kept encrypted and never shown again. With no login saved, the local test copy is used.

    Database copies

    Every time a workbook, NetBox or the CMDB is copied in, a new copy is kept. The newest copy of each rack is the one a result is compared with. Deleting a copy brings the one before it back.

    Copy a database in

    Read the manager's workbook (it is checked first, then Import), or copy NetBox or the ServiceNow CMDB in through the login under Connections (with no login, the local test copy). Each copy-in makes a new copy below.
