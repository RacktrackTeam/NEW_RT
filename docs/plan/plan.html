Video-first version: one video gives the rack, its exact match in the chosen database, and the drift

A plan for 2 Nov 2026. racktrack-app is the base, RT-EOS comes in whole, dark_mobile gives only its NetBox and CMDB code. Nothing has been changed, backed up or pushed yet.

01The pipeline

A video comes in. The rack is read: every unit, its occupancy, each device, its type, make and model, the rack id, the device ids and labels, and the phone position. All of it becomes one metadata card, ranked. The site's chosen database (Excel, NetBox or ServiceNow CMDB, more later) is copied into our own database, the 5 closest racks are shortlisted, each is scored on the card, and the exact match is tied to the scan or a person picks. Then the record is compared with the scan, the drift is shown, and every remaining feature runs on the same reading. No logical layer: no live switches or network reads.

04Finding the exact rack

StepRule
The 5 closest racksThe phone position is matched to the organization's sites (within 300 m, as today's location check does). The racks of the nearest site come first, then the next site. Within a site, racks are ordered by the site, building, floor, room and row in the record, matched against what the rack label and device ids say. The first five are the shortlist; the whole site stays searchable.
ScoringEach rack in the chosen database gets a score from the ranked evidence: rank 1 or an exact rank 2 label ends the search; rank 3 to 7 add up with the reader's confidence, as the two apps' ladders do today (exact 1.0, separators ignored 0.95, repaired by the site's label rule 0.85).
Exact matchOne rack and no other scores above the bar: rank 1, an exact unique rack label, one serial or asset tag, or three or more device ids that all name the same rack. Then the scan is tied to that record without a tap, as RT-EOS does since 25 Sep.
A person picksAnything less shows the ranked candidates with the evidence in words ("Matched by the label read off the rack"), and the person confirms, picks another or types the name. The choice is kept as rank 1 for the next scan of this rack.
No candidateThe rack is scanned and reported on its own, marked "not in the records", and a manager can add it to the dataset later.
